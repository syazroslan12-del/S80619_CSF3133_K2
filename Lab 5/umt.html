About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT), formerly known as Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM), is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science, located at Mengabang Telipot, Kuala Terengganu, providing facilities for both students and lecturers from the Fisheries and Marine Science programmes to conduct their practical sessions and research.

Eventually, the Faculty of Fisheries and Marine Science of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia (UPM) in Serdang was transferred to Kuala Terengganu, and the branch campus was renamed Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT) in June 1996. Later, the name of the faculty was changed to the Faculty of Applied Sciences and Technology.

On 5 May 1999, the Cabinet of Malaysia approved the establishment of Terengganu University College (KUT) as an associate campus of UPM. KUT was given autonomy on 1 May 2001 and was renamed the Malaysian University College of Science and Technology (KUSTEM) on 20 June 2001. On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM was given the status of a full-fledged university and rebranded as Universiti Malaysia Terengganu.
